Venue Info

Good to know before you go

8 pro courts under 200-ft shade runways, plus the Turtle Shack bar — 7 TVs, cold drinks, and concessions. One consistent set of rules across the whole venue: check in at Turtle Shack when required, and complete your waiver before you play.

FoodAny & all outside food is welcome — bring the cooler.
DrinksNo outside beverages, except coffee, smoothies, and water.
Not AllowedNo dogs, no glass, and no smoking anywhere at the venue.
ChairsChairs are welcome — get comfortable courtside.
Canopies & TentsAllowed, as long as they don't block walkways or others' views.
Before You PlayCheck in at Turtle Shack when required and complete your waiver first.
