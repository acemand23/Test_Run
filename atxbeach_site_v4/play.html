How it works

From reservation to first serve

Three quick steps and you're playing. New here? Open play is the easiest way to get a game going.

1

Reserve or drop in

Book a court by the hour through YourCourts, or grab a spot in an open play session — no full team required.

2

Check in & sign your waiver

Stop by the Turtle Shack to check in. Every player completes a waiver before stepping on the sand.

3

Play ball

Eight pro-grade courts under 200-ft shade runways. Grab water at the Turtle Shack and play.

Open play & court rentals

Whether you're rallying with friends or looking to meet new players, ATX Beach keeps the courts busy from morning to midnight.

Court reservations

  • Book any of 8 professional-grade sand courts by the hour.
  • Great for groups, leagues, birthday games, and practice.
  • Shaded by 200-ft runway structures — playable through Austin summers.

Open play

  • Drop in solo or with a partner and rotate into games.
  • All levels welcome — from first-timers to competitive players.
  • Check the live schedule in YourCourts for today's sessions.

What to bring

  • Outside food is welcome — bring a cooler. (No outside drinks except coffee, smoothies & water.)
  • Chairs are welcome; canopies are fine if they don't block walkways or views.
  • Sunscreen, water, and your A-game.
