Factory misprint · verified

The position block on the reverse should read MIDFIELDER. On this single card, the “IE” became “EI.” One transposed pair of letters — the kind of thing a spell-checker forgives and a collector never does.

Panini has never acknowledged the run. No second copy has surfaced. Which is exactly why a 20-cent common becomes a $350 grail: scarcity doesn't care whether the scarcity was on purpose.
