Aussie's Grill & Beach Bar — three directions

Three takes on a new home page. Each one uses the real hours, prices and specials from the current site, and each is built around the same three jobs a visitor has: join a league, enter a tournament, or book a court. Photos and the logo are Aussie's own, from the current site and Facebook page. League spot counts and the court grid are sample data.

Concept ANight Game

Dark, loud, scoreboard energy. Leads with a live "tonight" board: who's on the sand, what's on special, what's on the screens.

Best if the sports-bar crowd is the priority.

What all three fix

  • The three things people come to do are one tap from the top of the page, on a phone.
  • League nights show how many of the 8 spots are left, with a waitlist when full, instead of "pay and check whether you got in."
  • Court rental shows open hours and a running price, with league and tournament times already blocked.
  • Today's special and tonight's play update themselves from the day of the week.
  • Hours, address and phone are always in the same place.
