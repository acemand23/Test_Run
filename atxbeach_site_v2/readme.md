I am finally ready to attack this. Here is the big-picture recap of what we need cleaned up and built out for the ATX Beach website revamp. I have added ATX Beach's Marketing & Events Success Coordinator, Jadyen, to this thread.
The main goal is to make the website easier for customers to understand and easier for them to take action. Right now, we have too many offerings living in different systems, and customers are sometimes unsure where to register, book, or get details.

The website needs to become the clear front door for:

Book a Court

Join Open Play

Join Adult Training

Register for Tournaments

Learn about ATX Juniors

Host Private Events

Buy Memberships

Learn about Turtle Shack

View upcoming major events

Contact the right person/team

The biggest routing fix is that we need separate customer paths instead of one generic “Book” or “Register” flow.

Website CTA structure should be:

Book a Court → YourCourts

Join Open Play → YourCourts

Join Training / Clinics → TeamUp

Tournaments → VolleyballLife

Major Ticketed Events / Festivals → TicketSocket

Memberships → TeamUp

Private Events / Sponsors → Website inquiry form or CRM lead form

Important rule:

Open Play should live in YourCourts

Training and clinics should live in TeamUp

Tournaments should live in VolleyballLife

Major events like ATX Fit Fest should live in TicketSocket

We should not dual-home the same session in multiple systems unless it is only being used as a marketing/acquisition channel

Main site updates needed:

Homepage should have clearer action tiles/buttons

Adult Training needs to be more prominent

Memberships need a cleaner explanation and direct links

Open Play should have its own clean page

Tournaments need a stronger landing page with active events and registration links

Private Events should have a polished inquiry page

ATX Juniors needs a cleaner customer journey

Turtle Shack needs better placement, photos, and updated info

Policies should be easy to find and consistent across the site

Contact forms need to route clearly so leads are not getting lost

Current ATX Beach systems to account for:

YourCourts = court reservations, open play, check-in, waivers

TeamUp = adult training, clinics, memberships, long-term fitness/training funnel

VolleyballLife = tournament registrations

TicketSocket = major events and festival-style ticketing

Union POS = Turtle Shack sales

Mailchimp = email/SMS marketing

Copper = potential CRM for sponsors, partners, private events, and leads

Specific content/features we likely need:

“Book a Court” page

“Open Play” page

“Adult Training” page

“Memberships” page

“Tournaments” page

“Private Events” page

“ATX Juniors” page

“Turtle Shack” page

“Sponsors / Partners” page

“Policies / Venue Info” page

Cleaner homepage navigation

Mobile-friendly CTA bar

Better registration buttons throughout

Updated photos and optimized media

Clear waiver/check-in language

Better SEO/meta descriptions for major offerings

Lead capture forms for events, sponsors, private rentals, and training questions

Venue policy info to keep consistent:

Any & all outside food is allowed

No outside beverages allowed, except coffee, smoothies, and water

No dogs, glass, or smoking allowed at the venue

Chairs are welcome

Canopies and tents are allowed as long as they are not set up blocking walkways or customers’ view

Players should check in at Turtle Shack when required

Waivers need to be completed before participating

Performance note:

WP Engine flagged bandwidth, so we should optimize images/video and avoid hosting large videos directly on the site. YouTube/Vimeo embeds or lighter media handling would probably be better.

Big upcoming/strategic items the site should support:

TruVolley / TVR rating system rollout

TruVolley Texas multi-venue summer series

ATX Fit Fest — 5K & 10K + Wellness Expo

Adult training growth

ATX Juniors growth

Private event and sponsor lead capture

Turtle Shack visibility

Membership sales

Better tournament registration flow

Overall goal:

Make the website feel less like a static info page and more like the main ATX Beach customer hub. Customers should immediately know what they want to do, where to click, and how to register or contact us without having to text or email just to figure out the basics. We want the Homepage to feature 4 large, playing-card-like tiles. Play, Train, ATX Juniors, & Events are the 4 tile titles.
