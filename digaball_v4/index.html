Mission

Our mission is to train athletes to be the best at our sport, and to promote the sport so that our athletes have a future sport to play.

The sport

Three turns in the game.

  1. Early ’90s

    The boom

    Volleyball surges — packed gyms, packed beaches, a generation falls for the sport.

  2. 2000

    The collapse

    The pro and grassroots momentum stalls. The sport contracts and loses its footing.

  3. 2015

    The comeback

    Volleyball returns as the fastest-growing sport in collegiate history.
