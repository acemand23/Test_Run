Three decades of the game

’92

The Boom

Early-’90s beach volleyball explodes — pro tours, prime-time crowds, packed sand from coast to coast.

’00

The Collapse

By 2000 the pro circuit folds. The lights go down and the sport goes quiet for the better part of a decade.

’15

The Comeback

By 2015 it’s named the fastest-growing sport in collegiate history — and the next generation steps on the sand.
